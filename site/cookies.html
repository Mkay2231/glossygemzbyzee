Cookie policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and no other company's scripts run on this site. Your browser only stores what the shop needs to work.

What your browser stores

These stay on your device. They aren't sent to us except when you place an order or track one, and other websites can't read them.

NameWhat it's forHow long
ggz-bagThe pieces in your bagUntil you clear your browser storage
ggz-giftsGifts you have builtUntil you clear your browser storage
ggz-savedPieces you saved with the heartUntil you clear your browser storage
ggz-ordersLinks to orders you placed on this device, so you can track themUntil you clear your browser storage
ggz-storeA copy of the collection, so pages load fasterReplaced on each visit
ggz-last-orderYour order confirmation, if you refresh the pageUntil you close the tab

Cookies

The site sets one cookie, ggz_owner, and only for the store owner when she signs in to the dashboard. It keeps her signed in for up to 7 days. Customers never get it.

Do I need to accept anything?

No. Everything above is strictly necessary for the shop to work, so the law doesn't require consent for it, and we don't show a cookie banner.

Clearing it

You can clear this storage at any time in your browser's settings, usually under "Clear browsing data" or "Site settings". Your bag and saved pieces will then be empty.
