Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data we collect when you shop with us, why we need it, who we share it with, how long we keep it and the rights you have under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Who we are

GLOSSYGEMZ BY ZEE ENTERPRISES, trading as Glossy Gems by Zee, registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission of Nigeria under number 8636509, based in Magboro, Ogun State, Nigeria.

Email glossygemzbyzee@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp at 0907 274 7685.

We are the data controller for the personal data described here.

What we collect

We only ask for what we need to take, deliver and support your order.

  • When you place an order: your name, mobile number, email address, delivery address (street, city or town, local government area and state), and any delivery note or gift note you choose to write.
  • About your order: what you bought, the amounts, the order status and the date and time.
  • When you track an order by order number, we check the phone number you enter against the order. We don't store the lookup.
  • When you message us on WhatsApp or by email, we see what you send us there.

We don't collect payment card details. Payment is by bank transfer to our account, so your bank handles that information, not this website.

We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we don't build profiles of you.

Why we use it

  • To process, deliver and support your order, and to send you updates about it by email. This is necessary to perform our contract with you.
  • To keep records we're required to keep for tax and accounting. This is a legal obligation.
  • To answer questions or complaints you send us.

We don't send marketing emails, and we never sell or rent your personal data.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers that run the store for us, and only what each one needs:

  • Netlify, Inc. (United States) hosts the website and stores order records.
  • Resend, Inc. (United States) delivers our order emails, once they are switched on.
  • Google (Gmail) holds our business inbox, where we receive a notice of each new order and any emails you send us.
  • Delivery riders or couriers receive your name, phone number and delivery address to bring your order.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) carries any messages you choose to send us there, under its own privacy policy.

Netlify, Resend and Google store data outside Nigeria. We rely on their contractual data protection commitments to protect it, as the Nigeria Data Protection Act requires for transfers abroad. We may also share data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for up to six years after the order, because Nigerian tax and accounting rules require it. After that we delete them.

If you ask us to delete your details sooner, we remove your name, phone number, email and address from the order. We keep only what the law requires us to keep, such as the amounts and the items.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete your personal data, where the law doesn't require us to keep it;
  • stop or limit how we use it, or send it to you in a format you can reuse.

Email glossygemzbyzee@gmail.com with your order number. We'll reply within 30 days, usually much sooner. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission at ndpc.gov.ng.

Children

Our store is not meant for children. To place an order you must be 18 or older, or have permission from a parent or guardian. If we learn that a child has given us personal data without that permission, we'll delete it.

Keeping it safe

The website only works over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Order records can only be opened by the store owner, who signs in with a password. Each order's tracking page is protected by a private link or by your phone number.

Cookies and browser storage

We don't use tracking cookies. See our cookie policy for what your browser stores and why.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
