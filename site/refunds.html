Refund policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

We check every piece before it leaves us. If something isn't right when it arrives, we'll fix it.

What we cover

We replace or refund an item when it:

  • arrives damaged or faulty;
  • is not the item, size or option you ordered;
  • is missing from your order.

We don't accept returns for a change of mind. Please check sizes and photos before you order, or ask Zee on WhatsApp if you're unsure.

How to report a problem

  1. Contact us within 3 days of delivery on WhatsApp (0907 274 7685) or by email (glossygemzbyzee@gmail.com).
  2. Include your order number and clear photos or a short video showing the problem.
  3. Keep the item and its packaging until we've agreed what happens next.

What happens next

  • We'll reply within 2 working days and offer a replacement or a full refund for the affected item, whichever you prefer.
  • If we need the item back, we arrange and pay for the return.
  • Refunds go back to the bank account you paid from, within 7 working days of us agreeing the refund.
  • If the whole order was wrong, we also refund the delivery fee.

Cancelling before dispatch

You can cancel any order before we dispatch it and get a full refund of anything you've paid.

Money bouquets and gift items

If a money bouquet arrives with less cash than you paid for, we'll pay you the difference. Flowers and other gift items are covered if they arrive damaged or wrong.

Your legal rights

This policy is in addition to your rights under the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act, which protect you if goods are defective or not as described.
